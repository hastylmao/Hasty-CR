### **#1 - KataCR (wty-yy/KataCR)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**URL:** https://github.com/wty-yy/KataCR

**Approach:** Non-embedded AI using Offline Reinforcement Learning + Computer Vision (YOLOv8)

**Why It's #1:**
- **Academic Research Quality** - Published as a formal research paper at arXiv (April 2025)
- **Novel Methodology** - First non-embedded RL agent for Clash Royale that plays like humans (via visual input only)
- **Complete System** - End-to-end pipeline: perception → decision → action
- **Beats Built-in AI** - Successfully defeats 8000-rating AI opponents (10% win rate demonstrated)
- **Generative Dataset** - Created 150+ category object detection dataset with 6939 manually labeled validation images
- **Advanced Architecture** - Uses StARformer with spatial-temporal attention, Decision Transformer, and multi-agent setup
- **Open Source Everything** - Code, datasets, trained models, and videos all available
- **Real-time Performance** - 120ms decision time + 240ms perception fusion on laptop GPU

**Tech Stack:** JAX, PyTorch, PaddlePaddle, YOLOv8, PaddleOCR, Transformer architecture

**Last Updated:** 2024-2025 (Research paper published April 2025)

**Tech Stack:** Python, OpenCV (image recognition), PyAutoGUI
