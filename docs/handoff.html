Two traps that cost hours during this run. (1) PowerShell parses overnight_supervisor.ps1 at launch — editing it does nothing until the supervisor is restarted, and the running bot will silently keep the old flags. Verify with the process command line, not the file. (2) .py files reload per block, so Python edits take effect at the next block without a restart.

Known bugs — day-2 priorities

  1. Ally tower HP detection is broken (highest priority). The vendored detector reports our right princess tower at 0.00 while it sits at full 3346 HP — reproduced on multiple identical frames. A replacement bar reader (scripts/tower_hp.py) matched ground truth on two frames and failed on a third because the HP bar's vertical position shifts between matches (y 1177–1194 vs y 1160–1177). Widening the search band made it worse (read 0.966 for a true 0.369) by catching other blue UI. Suggested fix: anchor the ROI to a detected landmark — the tower sprite or level badge — instead of absolute pixels. Until this is fixed, defensive performance cannot be measured at all.
  2. End-of-match tower sampling. last_towers is captured on the final in-game frame, inside the end-of-match animation. Sample it a second or two earlier, or take the value from the last frame where battle_guard passed with bars present.
  3. pre_card_guard rejects ~149 decisions/night. The hand shifts between the model's decision frame (~1s stale) and the tap. The guard is correct and must not be weakened — it prevents playing the wrong card — but decision latency is the underlying cost.
  4. Model emits out-of-arena coordinates (observed (-2,28), (-2,33)). Now clamped and logged as CLAMP_OOB rather than raising.

Safety layer — do not weaken

  • battle_guard / validate_battle gate every card tap on a confirmed live battle (two live tower bars, ≥3 real cards in hand), re-checked before selecting a card and before placing it.
  • The runner never taps an unrecognised screen except a positively identified popup close button (popup_guard.py: colour + geometry + shape, validated against 172 archived screens).
  • Nothing taps shop, gem, or purchase controls. A Pass Royale offer popup was dismissed via its X during the run and the gem balance was verified unchanged at 990.
  • RECOVER relaunches Clash Royale if the screen stays unknown for 3 minutes (rate-limited to once per 5 minutes).

Methodology warnings

Outcomes are statistically underpowered at this cadence. Crowns arrived at ~0.7 per 5-match block; detecting even a 50% improvement needs roughly 80–95 blocks (400–475 matches) per configuration. The entire run was 36 blocks. Behaviour metrics (card counts, placement spread, conversion) are reliable because they count hundreds of events per block; win-rate comparisons across a few blocks are not.

Things that looked true and were not, during this run: that the hand-written policy beat the model (3 blocks of data, noise); that enemy tower HP was unreliable (compared two captures taken seconds apart — it is accurate mid-match); that a fast match was an opponent walkover (it was a 52-second 3-0 defeat). Validate on identical frames, and prefer results.py over derived metrics.

What was already tried

  • Hand-written policy driving decisions (--heuristic): scored worse than model+shims and turtled badly (194 defends vs 9 pushes in one block) before the gates were corrected. Left in the tree, disabled.
  • Raising Hog frequency: done and capped. ~64 elixir/match at 2.6 avg cost ≈ 24–32 cards; Hog is 1 of 8 and only reachable by playing the other three, so ~3–4/match is the physical ceiling. The bot is at it. More Hog is not available.
  • Replacing tower-HP perception: attempted and reverted (see Known bugs #1).

Suggested starting point

  1. Fix ally tower HP (landmark-anchored ROI), then re-measure defence — it is the only unmeasured half of the game.
  2. Use results.py as the sole success metric. Target: a first win, not crown counts.
  3. If the policy is the ceiling, note the checkpoint (StARformer_3L_v0.8_golem_ai) was trained against KataCR's scripted golem bot, not 5500-trophy ladder opponents — it is off-distribution by construction.
